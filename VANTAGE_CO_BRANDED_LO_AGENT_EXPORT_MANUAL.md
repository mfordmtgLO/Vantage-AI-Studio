## 7. Customizable Lead Capture Form & AI 2nd Brain Cognitive Lead Triage

The Vantage Real Estate GeoMap module features an advanced, customizable **Lead Capture Form** component (`RealEstateLeadCaptureForm.tsx`) powered by the **AI 2nd Brain Cognitive Lead Triage Engine** (`leadCaptureEmailService.ts` and `/api/realestate/lead-capture-email`).

When a prospective homebuyer submits questions, notes, or showing requests for any property listing, the system executes an automated workflow:
1. **Simultaneous Dual Email Dispatch:** Instantly notifies the Loan Officer (`fordmj@gmail.com`) and Real Estate Agent (`kanndice@cascadepremier.com`). If in "Solo LO Mode", agent routing gracefully deactivates.
2. **AI 2nd Brain Text Decomposition:** Gemini parses the raw submission text into two distinct professional operational domains:
   - **Loan Officer Responsibilities:** Loan programs (USDA 100%, FHA 3.5%, HomeReady, Lakeview National DPA, OHCS Flex Lending), down payment grants ($15,400+ max), credit scores, pre-approval timeline, monthly payment PITI breakdown, DTI ratios, debts, collections, underwriting, documentation (W-2s, 1040s), income verification, escrows, and interest rates.
