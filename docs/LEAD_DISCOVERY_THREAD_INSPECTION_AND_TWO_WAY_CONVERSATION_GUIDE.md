# 📚 Lead Discovery Scrape Thread & Two-Way Conversation Training Manual

## 🎯 Executive Overview
This guide trains Loan Officers and Administrators on locating, inspecting, and engaging with **high-intent leads** discovered during automated daily forum and chat board sweeps across Oregon and all 50 states. It covers:
1. Viewing the original **scraped source comment thread** directly inside the dashboard.
2. Obtaining the **direct URL link** to the original post on Reddit, BiggerPockets, Housing Chat Boards, or PNW Real Estate Blogs.
3. Engaging in **two-way conversations** with multi-channel routing (**SMS**, **Gmail**, and **AI 2nd Brain Direct Anchors**).
4. Reviewing **SMS interaction histories** and managing pipeline status.

---

## 🧭 Step-by-Step Navigation

### 1. Opening the Lead Discovery Studio
1. Open the **Admin Workspace** (`?view=dashboard` or `?mobile_admin=true`).
2. Navigate to:
   * **Desktop Mode**: Click **"Lead Discovery Studio"** from the navigation sidebar or top menu.
   * **Mobile Admin Dashboard**: Tap the **"Oregon Lead Discovery"** (or **"⚡ 50-State Processor"**) tab in the horizontal menu bar.

## 🔍 How to View the Source Comment Thread & Direct URL

Every discovered prospect card is paired with the exact website or chat board thread from which the inquiry was extracted.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [Reddit r/Bend]  •  Bend, OR  •  🔥 94% Intent Score   [🌟 New Scrape]           |
|  "Rent just jumped $250/mo. Anyone used the Oregon OHCS Flex Grant for zero down?"|
|                                                                                   |
|  [By u/BendOutdoorBuyer] [SMS History] [⚡ Dual SMS+Gmail] [💬 Join Convo] [↗]  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                                                               ^
                                                      Click External Link Button [↗]
```

### Step 1: Click the External Link Button (`↗`)
On the bottom-right corner of any lead card, click the **External Link icon (`↗`)**.

### Step 2: In-App Live Website & Thread Viewer Modal
This opens the **`ThreadWebsiteViewerModal`**, an embedded browser simulation that renders:
* **Interactive Address Bar (Top)**: Displays the full, secure SSL URL (e.g., `https://reddit.com/r/Bend/comments/1f4k9a/oregon_first_time_buyer_grants/`).
* **One-Click Copy Link Button**: Click **"Copy Link"** in the address bar to copy the exact URL to your clipboard for external browsing.
* **AI 2nd Brain Direct Comment Anchor**: A persistent badge showing the unique `MessageThreadID` (e.g., `#th_bend_outdoor_88`) and `TargetCommentID` (e.g., `#cmt_f4k9a_01`) linking directly to the borrower's query.
* **Full Article & Community Comments Section**: Scroll down to view the conversation context, upvotes, and community replies.
* **Direct Comment Posting**: Type in the comment box at the bottom and click **"Post Comment"** to reply directly into the thread as **Mike Ford (Senior Loan Officer)**.

## 💬 Managing Two-Way Conversations in the Dashboard

### 1. Filtering by "Active Two-Way Conversation"
* In the filter bar at the top of the **Lead Discovery Studio**, click the **"💬 Active 2-Way"** filter chip.
* This filters out uncontacted leads and isolates all borrower threads where active two-way communication has commenced.
* You can also change the status dropdown on any lead card directly to **`💬 Active 2-Way`**.

### 2. Launching the AI Reply Studio ("Join Conversation")
Click the green **"Join Conversation"** button (`MessageSquare` icon) on any lead card to open the **AI Reply Studio Modal**:

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Original Renter Query** | Displays the borrower's exact quoted question and sentiment. |
| **2nd Brain Routing Anchor** | Verifies the pinned `MessageThreadID` for continuous CRM synchronization. |
| **Channel Selector** | Toggle between **⚡ Dual (SMS + Gmail)**, **📱 SMS Only**, or **✉️ Gmail Draft Only**. |
| **Tone Variant Selector** | Switch between *Warm & Empathetic*, *Analytical Numbers Breakdown*, and *Quick 2-Minute Pre-Approval Invite*. |
| **GeoMap Rate Card Attachment** | Attaches live 2-1 buydown calculations, monthly payment comparisons, and zero-down grant eligibility. |

## 📱 Reviewing SMS History & Dialog Logs

1. Click the **"SMS History"** button (`Clock/History` icon) on any lead card.
2. The **SMS Interaction Drawer** displays:
   * Chronological log of all outbound texts sent to the prospect.
   * Inbound SMS responses received via Twilio Relay.
   * Delivery timestamps, carrier routing confirmations, and matched property scenarios.
